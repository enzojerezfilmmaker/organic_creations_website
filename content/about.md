Organic Creations Group is an artistic collective created in 2014, conformed mainly by artists and technicians of the cinematographic and theatrical area resident in Berlin. Gradually, in these five years of existence, our group derived in a community of artists and technicians of diverse areas. In a process of natural ramification of connections abroad, we grew organically to be a wider community. This year 2019 we created an association registered in Germany, called Organic Creations Group e.V. and among our main objectives are:

	•	To give support to artists and technicians of different areas of culture at national and international level.
	•	To serve as a connecting platform between artists and technicians worldwide, who primarily wish to carry out activities oriented towards qualitative excellence in their respective arts and professions.
